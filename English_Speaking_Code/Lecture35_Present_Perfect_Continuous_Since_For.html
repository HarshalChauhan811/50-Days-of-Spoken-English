Premium notes • Present Perfect Continuous with Since & For
PRESENT PERFECT CONTINUOUS HAS/HAVE BEEN V4 / ING SINCE FOR POSITIVE NEGATIVE INTERROGATIVE WH-QUESTIONS
Core idea

Present Perfect Continuous is used in the lesson for an action that has been continuing up to the present, especially when a duration/time is given.

जब कोई काम कुछ समय से लगातार चल रहा है और उसका time/duration दिया गया है, तब इस lesson में Present Perfect Continuous का use किया गया है.

I have been teaching for two hours. • She has been sleeping for two hours.

1. Present Continuous vs Present Perfect Continuous

Present Continuous

I am teaching.

मैं पढ़ा रहा हूँ।

Action present में चल रहा है.

Present Perfect Continuous

I have been teaching for two hours.

मैं 2 घंटे से पढ़ा रहा हूँ।

Action + duration/time.

Lesson distinction: सिर्फ current action हो → am/is/are + V4.

Current action के साथ duration/time दिया हो → has/have been + V4 + since/for + time.

2. Main Structure

Positive Structure

Subject + has / have + been + V4 (ing) + Object + since / for + Time

SubjectHelping VerbExample
He / She / It / Singular Namehas beenHe has been sleeping.
I / We / You / Theyhave beenI have been teaching.
Memory formula

Singular → HAS BEEN

I / We / You / They → HAVE BEEN

Then main verb → V4 / -ing

3. Since vs For — सबसे Important Concept

SINCE = Point of Time

जब starting point fixed हो.

since Sunday

since morning

since April

since 2015

FOR = Period of Time

जब duration दी हो, लेकिन exact starting point न दिया हो.

for two hours

for four months

for two years

for three days

Shortcut

SINCE → “कब से?” यानी fixed starting point.

FOR → “कितने समय से?” यानी duration/period.

4. Positive Sentences — Step by Step

वह 2 घंटे से सो रहा है.

He → has been → sleep + ing → for two hours

He has been sleeping for two hours.

मैं Sunday से पढ़ा रहा हूँ.

I → have been → teach + ing → since Sunday

I have been teaching since Sunday.

मैं जा रहा हूँ. → I am going.

मैं 2 घंटे से जा रहा हूँ. → I have been going for two hours.

मैं खा रहा हूँ. → I am eating.

मैं 4 बजे से खा रहा हूँ. → I have been eating since 4 o'clock.

5. Practical Positive Practice

He has been reading a book since morning.

वह सुबह से किताब पढ़ रहा है।

We have been travelling for four months.

हम चार महीनों से यात्रा कर रहे हैं।

They have been waiting for us since 2015.

वे 2015 से हमारा इंतजार कर रहे हैं।

Why?

morning → fixed point/part of day → since

four months → duration → for

2015 → fixed year → since

7. Negative Sentences

Structure

Subject + has / have + not + been + V4 + Object + since / for + Time

I have not been eating food for two hours.

मैं 2 घंटे से खाना नहीं खा रहा हूँ।

She has not been singing a song for an hour.

वह 1 घंटे से गाना नहीं गा रही है।

We have not been going to school for three days.

हम तीन दिनों से school नहीं जा रहे हैं।

I have not been reading the English newspaper since last week.

मैं पिछले सप्ताह से English newspaper नहीं पढ़ रहा हूँ।

Seema has not been eating ice cream for two days.

सीमा दो दिनों से ice cream नहीं खा रही है।

Negative pattern

बस has/have के बाद NOT add करें.

I have been reading. → I have not been reading.

She has been singing. → She has not been singing.

8. Interrogative Sentences

Structure

Has / Have + Subject + been + V4 + Object + since / for + Time?

Has the gardener been watering the plants for two hours?

क्या माली 2 घंटे से पौधों को पानी दे रहा है?

Has she been reading a book since morning?

क्या वह सुबह से पुस्तक पढ़ रही है?

Have they been cooking in the kitchen for one month?

क्या वे एक महीने से kitchen में खाना बना रहे हैं?

Has Ramesh been going to the gym since last week?

क्या रमेश पिछले week से gym जा रहा है?

Have they been working in the garden for three days?

क्या वे तीन दिनों से garden में काम कर रहे हैं?

9. Interrogative Negative Sentences

Structure

Has / Have + Subject + not + been + V4 + Object + since / for + Time?

क्या तुम 2 घंटे से serial नहीं देख रहे हो?

Has she not been playing the guitar for two weeks?

क्या वह दो हफ्तों से guitar नहीं बजा रही है?

Have they not been coming to coaching for the last month?

क्या वे पिछले महीने से coaching नहीं आ रहे हैं?

Have we not been exercising for three months?

क्या हम तीन महीनों से exercise नहीं कर रहे हैं?

Have the players not been playing cricket since last year?

क्या खिलाड़ी पिछले साल से cricket नहीं खेल रहे हैं?

11. One Sentence → Multiple Forms

Base: “मैं 2 घंटे से पढ़ रहा हूँ.”

1. I have been studying for two hours.

2. I have not been studying for two hours.

3. Have I been studying for two hours?

4. Have I not been studying for two hours?

5. Why have I been studying for two hours?

Practice principle

एक ही sentence को positive → negative → interrogative → interrogative negative → WH में बोलने की practice करें. इससे structure naturally याद होता है.

12. V4 / ING — Quick Reminder

V4 = Verb + ING

teach → teaching

sleep → sleeping

read → reading

run → running

watch → watching

play → playing

Since / For at a glance

SINCE: since Sunday, since morning, since April, since 2015.

FOR: for two hours, for four months, for two years, for three days.

Top mistakes

I am reading for two hours. ❌ when the intended lesson pattern is an action continuing with duration

I have been reading for two hours. ✅

He has been sleeping since two hours. ❌

He has been sleeping for two hours. ✅

She have been studying. ❌

She has been studying. ✅

They has been working. ❌

They have been working. ✅

Final takeaway

Present Perfect Continuous = HAS/HAVE + BEEN + V4 + SINCE/FOR + TIME

Since = fixed starting point   |   For = duration/period

सबसे पहले subject पहचानें, फिर has/have, फिर been, फिर V4, और अंत में सही since/for + time.
