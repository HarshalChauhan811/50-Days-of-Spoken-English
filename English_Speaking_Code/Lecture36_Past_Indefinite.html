Core idea

Past Indefinite is used to talk about a completed action, past event, or past experience.

जब हमें बीते हुए समय की किसी घटना, क्रिया या completed action के बारे में बताना हो, तब Past Indefinite का use किया जाता है.

I woke up at 6 o'clock. • I went for a walk. • I took breakfast.

1. Past Indefinite — पहचान

Common Hindi endings in the lesson include forms such as या / ए / ई / था, but the more useful approach is to identify whether the action/event belongs to the past.

Examples: गया, देखा, पिया, किया, बुलाया.

Most important skill

Past conversation में completed actions बताने के लिए Past Indefinite बहुत frequently useful है. सबसे पहले यह तय करें कि action past में completed है या नहीं.

2. Positive Structure

Structure

Subject + V2 + Object

Positive Past Indefinite में main verb की second form आती है.

He went.

वह गया।

I saw him.

मैंने उसे देखा।

We drank water.

हमने पानी पिया।

He bothered me.

उसने मुझे परेशान किया।

Raju called me.

राजू ने मुझे phone किया।

3. V1 → V2 → V3: Why Verb Forms Matter

Past Indefinite depends on V2

अगर verb की second form याद नहीं है, तो Past Indefinite के sentences बनाना मुश्किल होगा. इसलिए common verbs के V1/V2/V3 forms revise करना जरूरी है.

4. Five Basic Past Indefinite Sentences

  1. वह गया.
  2. मैंने उसे देखा.
  3. हमने पानी पिया.
  4. उसने मुझे परेशान किया.
  5. राजू ने मुझे फोन किया.
Answers
1. He went.
2. I saw him.
3. We drank water.
4. He bothered me.
5. Raju called me.

5. Past Routine — “My Yesterday's Routine”

Routine in the past

जब कल/पिछले दिन की routine बतानी हो, तो हर completed action को Past Indefinite में express किया जा सकता है.

Hindi flow: मैं सुबह 5 बजे उठा → walk पर गया → नहाया → तैयार हुआ → पूजा की → breakfast किया.

I woke up at 5 o'clock in the morning yesterday.

Then I went for a walk.

After that, I took a bath.

Then I got ready.

After that, I worshipped.

Then I took my breakfast.

Connectors for a past routine

Then • After that • Yesterday • In the morning

Routine के हर action में सही V2 use करें.

6. Infinitive with Past Indefinite

Purpose pattern

Main past action + to + V1

“करने के लिए” वाले purpose को express करने के लिए source lesson में to + V1 pattern दिया गया है.

He went to cook food.

वह खाना बनाने के लिए गया।

He called you to return your money.

उसने तुम्हारे पैसे लौटाने के लिए तुम्हें phone किया।

I worked to earn money.

मैंने पैसे कमाने के लिए काम किया।

We donated money to save him.

हमने उसे बचाने के लिए पैसे दान किए।

How to build long sentences

पहले main action पकड़ें → फिर purpose जोड़ें.

He went + to cook food

I worked + to earn money

Read: V1 = /रीड/, V2 = /रेड/.

8. Negative Sentences — DID NOT + V1

Structure

Subject + did not + V1 + Object

Short form: didn't + V1

Golden rule

Positive में V2 आता है, लेकिन negative में did not आने के बाद main verb V1 में चली जाती है.

I went. ✅ → I did not go. ✅

I did not went. ❌

I did not tell him.

मैंने उसे नहीं बताया।

He did not invite me to his wedding.

उसने मुझे अपनी शादी में नहीं बुलाया।

We did not play cricket properly yesterday.

हमने कल ठीक से cricket नहीं खेला।

You did not help me at home.

तुमने घर पर मेरी मदद नहीं की।

They did not do this work.

उन्होंने यह काम नहीं किया।

9. Negative Practice

  1. मैंने खाना नहीं खाया.
  2. उसने पैसे नहीं दिए.
  3. राम ने दूध नहीं पिया.
  4. मम्मी ने सब्जी नहीं बनाई.
  5. Teacher ने question नहीं पूछा.
Answers
1. I did not eat food.
2. He did not give money.
3. Ram did not drink milk.
4. Mom did not prepare vegetables.
5. The teacher did not ask a question.

10. Interrogative Sentences

Structure

Did + Subject + V1 + Object?

Did you do your homework?

क्या तुमने homework किया?

Did he answer?

क्या उसने जवाब दिया?

Did you make tea?

क्या आपने चाय बनाई?

Did they ask for help?

क्या उन्होंने मदद मांगी?

Did he go to the market?

क्या वह market गया?

Question में did आने के बाद main verb V1 रहती है.

Did he go? ✅   |   Did he went? ❌

11. Interrogative Negative

Structure

Did + Subject + not + V1 + Object?

Did he not go to school?

क्या वह school नहीं गया?

Did he not ask for help?

क्या उसने मदद नहीं मांगी?

Did they not tell the truth?

क्या उन्होंने सच नहीं बताया?

Did you not work?

क्या तुमने काम नहीं किया?

Did Ramesh not deposit the fee?

क्या रमेश ने fee नहीं भरी?

12. WH-Questions

Structure

WH-word + did + Subject + V1 + Object?

HindiEnglish
तुमने कल क्या खाया?What did you eat yesterday?
वह school क्यों गया?Why did he go to school?
तुमने उसे कब देखा?When did you see him?

WH-word पहले → did → subject → V1

13. Full Four-Pattern Practice

Base: “मैंने काम किया.”

Positive: I worked.

Negative: I did not work.

Interrogative: Did I work?

Interrogative Negative: Did I not work?

WH: Why did I work?

One big rule

Positive → V2

Negative / Question → DID + V1

Final takeaway

Past Indefinite = completed past action.

Positive → V2 | Negative → did not + V1 | Question → did + V1

सबसे जरूरी distinction: DID आने के बाद main verb कभी V2 नहीं — V1.
