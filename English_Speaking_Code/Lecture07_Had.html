3. Have/Has ka Past — HAD

Ab main topic start hota hai: Present ke have / has ka Past form had hota hai.

Definition

HAD = Past mein kisi person / thing / noun ke paas koi cheez hona

Basic Structure

Subject + HAD + Object

Example ideas: bachpan mein cycle thi, kisi ke paas cows thi, dadaji ke paas turban tha—yaani possession Past mein exist karti thi.

4. Had — First Practice

HindiEnglish
मेरे पास खिलौने थे।I had toys.
मेरे पास साइकिल थी।I had a bike.
मेरे पास दोस्त थे।I had friends.
मेरे पास गाँव का खाना था।I had village food.
मेरे पास एक कुत्ता था।I had a dog.
Quick Recall

Present: I have a bike.

Past: I had a bike.

5. Had — Different Subjects

Past possession mein subject singular ya plural ho, had same rehta hai.

I → HAD
You → HAD
He / She → HAD
We / They → HAD
HindiEnglish
उसके पास लैपटॉप था।He had a laptop.
हमारे पास आखिरी मौका था।We had a last chance.
उनके पास ऊँट थे।They had camels.
राजू के पास गाँव की जिम्मेदारी थी।Raju had responsibility of the village.
तुम्हारे पास English की किताब थी।You had an English book.
Golden Rule

Past Possession → Every Subject + HAD

7. Past Negative — Did Not Have

Jab Past mein kisi ke paas koi cheez nahi thi, to lecture mein did not have ka pattern sikhaya gaya.

Negative Formula

Subject + DID NOT + HAVE + Object

Critical Rule

Past negative mein did aane ke baad had use nahi karna hai. Main verb have form mein rehta hai.

HAD → DID NOT HAVE

Short Form

Did not → Didn't

I didn't have books.

8. Past Negative — Examples

HindiEnglish
मेरे पास किताबें नहीं थीं।I did not have books.
उसके पास दूसरा मौका नहीं था।He did not have another chance.
उनके पास कार की चाबी नहीं थी।They did not have the car key.
लोगों के पास अच्छा नेता नहीं था।People did not have a good leader.
Compare

Positive → He had a chance.

Negative → He did not have a chance.

9. Infinitive — Advanced Negative Sentences

Ab sentence ko advanced level par le jaaya gaya. “करने के लिए” batane ke liye to + Verb (First Form) use hota hai.

Infinitive Pattern

Subject + did not have + Object + to + Verb (First Form)

Sentence Breakdown

Main Part: Subject + did not have + Object

Purpose Part: to + Verb (First Form)

HindiEnglish
मेरे पास खाने के लिए कुछ नहीं था।I did not have anything to eat.
उसके पास पढ़ने के लिए किताब नहीं थी।He did not have a book to read.
हमारे पास पीने के लिए पानी नहीं था।We did not have water to drink.
मुन्नी के पास बात करने के लिए मोबाइल नहीं था।Munni did not have a mobile to talk.
Alternative Form Taught

“मेरे पास खाने के लिए कुछ नहीं था” ko lecture mein alternate structure se bhi explain kiya gaya:

I had nothing to eat.

10. Infinitive Rule — Remember

To

Infinitive marker

Verb

First form

Purpose

क्या करने के लिए?

to eat = खाने के लिए   |   to read = पढ़ने के लिए   |   to drink = पीने के लिए   |   to talk = बात करने के लिए

11. Extra Negative Practice

HindiEnglish
राम और श्याम के पास टिकट नहीं था।Ram and Shyam did not have tickets.
मेरे दोस्त के पास बाइक नहीं थी।My friend did not have a bike.

There Was Alternative

“गाँव में बिजली नहीं थी” ko lecture mein there was structure ke through bhi dikhaya gaya:

There was no electricity in the village.

12. Interrogative — Did + Have

Past possession ka question banane ke liye did ko sentence ki beginning mein le aao.

Interrogative Formula

DID + Subject + HAVE + Object?

Never Forget

Did + Subject + HAVE

Question mein had nahi; have aayega.

13. Interrogative — Examples

HindiEnglish
क्या तुम्हारे पास राजू का नंबर था?Did you have Raju's number?
क्या उनके पास सोने की ईंट थी?Did they have a golden brick?
क्या उसके पास English का ज्ञान था?Did he have English knowledge?
क्या हमारे पास आधुनिक हथियार थे?Did we have modern weapons?
Question Transformation

You had Raju's number.

→ Did you have Raju's number?

15. Interrogative Negative

Ab question ke saath negative meaning bhi add karna hai.

Question + Negative Formula

DID + Subject + NOT + HAVE + Object?

HindiEnglish
क्या मेरे पास अवसर नहीं था?Did I not have a chance?
क्या तुम्हारे माता-पिता के पास कार नहीं थी?Did your parents not have a car?
क्या वैज्ञानिकों के पास data नहीं था?Did scientists not have data?
क्या शिक्षकों के पास सामग्री नहीं थी?Did teachers not have material?
क्या दुकानदार के पास change नहीं था?Did the shopkeeper not have change?
Formation Order

DID → Subject → NOT → HAVE → Object?

16. WH-Questions with Had

Past possession se related WH-question mein WH word ke baad did aata hai, phir subject, aur phir have.

What
Where
Which
Who
Why
How
Whose
What type / Which
WH Formula

WH + DID + Subject + HAVE + ...?

HindiEnglish
तुम्हारे पास क्या था?What did you have?
उसके पास किसका फोन था?Whose phone did he have?
राम के पास किस रंग की कार थी?Which color car did Ram have?
रोहन के पास कौन सा मोबाइल था?Which mobile did Rohan have?
तुम्हारे पास कौन सा document था?Which document did you have?
WH Rule

WH word सबसे पहले आएगा। उसके बाद did, फिर subject और फिर have.

What + did + you + have?

17. Had — Complete Pattern Map

PatternFormulaExample
PositiveS + had + OHe had a laptop.
NegativeS + did not have + OHe did not have a laptop.
InterrogativeDid + S + have + O?Did he have a laptop?
Interrogative NegativeDid + S + not + have + O?Did he not have a laptop?
WH QuestionWH + did + S + have + O?What did he have?
One-Line Memory

Positive → HAD

Negative → DID NOT HAVE

Question → DID + HAVE

WH → WH + DID + HAVE

18. Paragraph Translation — Hindi → English

Ab lecture mein एक paragraph diya gaya jisme was / were aur had dono concepts revise karaye gaye. Important point: paragraph ko sirf likhna nahi, bolkar translate karne ki practice bhi karni hai.

Paragraph — Model Translation

कल मैं बहुत खुश था।

Yesterday I was very happy.

मेरा भाई घर पर था।

My brother was at home.

मेरी बहन स्कूल में थी।

My sister was in the school.

हम सब पार्क में थे।

We were all in the park.

मौसम बहुत अच्छा था।

The weather was very cool / pleasant.

मेरे दोस्त वहाँ थे।

My friends were also there.

हम सब साथ में बहुत खुश थे।

We were very happy together.

रात को मैं थोड़ा थका हुआ था।

I was a little tired at night.

लेकिन मेरा दिन बहुत अच्छा था।

But my day was very fine.

हमारे शिक्षक भी पार्क में थे।

Our teachers were also in the park.

सभी बच्चे खेल के लिए तैयार थे।

All children were ready for the game.

Activity

Isi pattern ki practice ke liye given sentences ko English mein convert karo. Focus rakho ki Past state/location ke liye was/were aur Past possession ke liye had identify ho.
