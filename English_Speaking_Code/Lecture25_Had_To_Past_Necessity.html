Lecture Focus: आज का main topic Had To है। इसका प्रयोग past में किसी काम के करना पड़ा / करना पड़ता था वाले sense को express करने के लिए किया जाता है। Lecture में positive, negative, interrogative, interrogative-negative, WH-questions, paragraph translation और 10 useful verbs cover किए गए हैं।

1. Had To — Basic Meaning

Meaning

Had to का प्रयोग past में किसी काम की necessity/compulsion बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Hindi sense: करना पड़ा / करना पड़ता था

Subject + had to + V1 + Object

Core Examples

HindiEnglish
उसे भागना पड़ा।He had to run.
मुझे अंग्रेजी सीखनी पड़ी।I had to learn English.
हमें नाचना पड़ा।We had to dance.
उन्हें यहाँ आना पड़ा।They had to come here.
राजू को घर छोड़ना पड़ा।Raju had to leave home.
Easy identification: अगर Hindi sentence में past necessity का sense है— “करना पड़ा / करनी पड़ी / करने पड़े / करना पड़ता था”—तो lesson के अनुसार had to का pattern सोचो।

3. Had To — Negative

जब meaning हो काम नहीं करना पड़ा, तो negative sentence में did not have to का प्रयोग होता है।

Subject + did not have to + V1 + Object
Important: Negative में had to नहीं लगाना है। Past negative pattern: did not have to + V1.
HindiEnglish
मुझे स्कूल नहीं जाना पड़ा।I did not have to go to school.
मुझे फिर से परीक्षा नहीं देनी पड़ी।I did not have to give the exam again.
उन्हें आज खाना नहीं बनाना पड़ा।They did not have to cook food today.
उसे लोगों से बात नहीं करनी पड़ी।He did not have to talk to people.
तुम्हें झपकी नहीं लेनी पड़ी।You did not have to take a nap.

4. Negative Practice

Try first:

  1. मुझे फिर से परीक्षा नहीं देनी पड़ी।
  2. उन्हें आज खाना नहीं बनाना पड़ा।
  3. उसे लोगों से बात नहीं करनी पड़ी।
  4. तुम्हें झपकी नहीं लेनी पड़ी।

Answers:

  1. I did not have to give the exam again.
  2. They did not have to cook food today.
  3. He did not have to talk to people.
  4. You did not have to take a nap.

5. Interrogative — Did ... Have To?

Question बनाने के लिए Did को शुरुआत में रखो। इसके बाद subject, have to, V1 और object आता है।

Did + Subject + have to + V1 + Object?
HindiEnglish
क्या तुम्हें उन्हें बुलाना पड़ा?Did you have to call them?
क्या उसे सुबह कपड़े धोने पड़े?Did he have to wash clothes in the morning?
क्या राजू को पैसे कमाने के लिए शहर जाना पड़ा?Did Raju have to go to the city to earn money?
क्या हमें नया मोबाइल खरीदना पड़ा?Did we have to buy a new mobile?
Two verbs in one sentence: “go to the city to earn money” में दूसरे verb से पहले to + V1 आता है।

6. To vs For — Important Grammar Point

जब purpose/action बताना हो, तो lesson में यह distinction बताया गया है:

to + V1 → to earn, to learn, to buy, to go
for + V4 (-ing) → for earning, for learning, for buying
Did Raju have to go to the city to earn money?
राजू को पैसे कमाने के लिए शहर जाना पड़ा?

7. Interrogative Negative

Did + Subject + not + have to + V1 + Object?
HindiEnglish
क्या तुम्हें किताब नहीं खरीदनी पड़ी?Did you not have to buy a book?
क्या उसे क्रिकेट नहीं खेलना पड़ा?Did he not have to play cricket?
क्या उन्हें घर नहीं बनाना पड़ा?Did they not have to build a house?
क्या मुन्नी को लोगों के सामने नहीं नाचना पड़ा?Did Munni not have to dance in front of people?

Phrase Focus: In Front Of

in front of = के सामने
dance in front of people

8. WH-Questions

WH-question में WH-word सबसे पहले आएगा, फिर did + subject + have to + V1।

WH-word + did + Subject + have to + V1 + Object?
HindiEnglish
उसे पुलिस को सूचना क्यों देनी पड़ी?Why did he have to inform the police?
तुम्हें दिल्ली से आगरा क्यों जाना पड़ा?Why did you have to go to Agra from Delhi?
जानवरों को जंगल छोड़ना कब पड़ा?When did animals have to leave the forest?
उसे नौकरी क्यों बदलनी पड़ी?Why did he have to change the job?
उसे पैसे क्यों उधार लेने पड़े?Why did he have to borrow money?
Lend vs Borrow:
lend = उधार देना
borrow = उधार लेना

18. Important Grammar Rules

Rule 1

Positive past necessity → had to + V1

Rule 2

Negative → did not have to + V1

Rule 3

Question → Did + subject + have to + V1?

Rule 4

WH-question → WH + did + subject + have to + V1?

Common mistake: Negative/question में had to को directly carry मत करो। Standard lesson pattern में did आने के बाद have to रखा जाता है।

19. Final Master Map

Past Necessity

Had to + V1

I had to go.

Past No Necessity

Did not have to + V1

I did not have to go.

Past Question

Did ... have to + V1?

Did you have to go?
One-Line Memory:
“करना पड़ा / करना पड़ता था” → Had To
“नहीं करना पड़ा” → Did Not Have To
“क्या करना पड़ा?” → Did ... Have To?
“क्यों/कहाँ/कब करना पड़ा?” → WH + Did ... Have To?
