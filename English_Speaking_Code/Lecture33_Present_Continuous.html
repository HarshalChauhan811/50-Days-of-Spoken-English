Core idea

Present Continuous is used when an action is happening/continuing in the present.

जब कोई व्यक्ति अभी या वर्तमान समय में कोई काम कर रहा है, तब Present Continuous का use किया जाता है.

I am teaching. • You are learning. • They are speaking English.

1. Present Continuous का Concept

Present = वर्तमान समय

Continuous = काम जारी है / चल रहा है

I am reading. = मैं पढ़ रहा हूँ।

She is singing. = वह गा रही है।

They are learning English. = वे English सीख रहे हैं।

Compare with Present Indefinite
Present IndefinitePresent Continuous
Habit / routine / factAction happening now / currently
I go to school.I am going to school.
She plays cricket.She is playing cricket.

2. Present Continuous — Main Structure

Positive Structure

Subject + am / is / are + V4 (verb + ing) + Object

Lesson terminology: V4 = verb का -ing form.

SubjectHelping VerbExample
IamI am reading.
He / She / It / Singular NameisHe is going. / She is singing. / Mohan is coming.
We / You / They / PluralareWe are learning. / You are speaking. / They are playing.
One-line memory

I → AM   |   He/She/It/Name → IS   |   We/You/They/Plural → ARE

3. Subject पहचानना

जो व्यक्ति/वस्तु action कर रही है, वही Subject है.

I am teaching. → “I” is the subject.

You are learning. → “You” is the subject.

She is singing. → “She” is the subject.

They are coming. → “They” is the subject.

Fast method

Sentence में पूछो: “कौन काम कर रहा है?” जो answer मिले, वही subject.

4. Positive Sentences — Step by Step

मैं पढ़ा रहा हूँ.

Subject = I → helping verb = am → teach + ing = teaching

I am teaching.

वह गाना गा रही है.

Subject = she → helping verb = is → sing + ing = singing

She is singing a song.

मैं एक किताब पढ़ रहा हूँ.

Subject = I → am → read + ing

I am reading a book.

5. He / She / It / Name के साथ IS

He is going.

वह (male) जा रहा है।

She is going.

वह (female) जा रही है।

It is going.

यह/वह non-living context में जा रहा है।

Mohan is going.

मोहन जा रहा है।

Sita is going.

सीता जा रही है।

Ram is going.

राम जा रहा है।

Present Continuous में singular subject देखकर is चुनना है, फिर main verb का -ing form लगाना है.

वे घर सजा रहे हैं।

7. Present Continuous के Uses

1. Action happening now

I am completing my homework.

मैं अभी homework कर रहा हूँ।

2. Temporary action

I am writing.

मैं इस समय लिख रहा हूँ।

3. Current / these-days activity

I am learning English.

आजकल मैं English सीख रहा हूँ।

He is running.

वह दौड़ रहा है।

She is dancing.

वह नाच रही है।

I am completing my homework.

मैं homework पूरा कर रहा हूँ।

8. Negative Sentences

Structure

Subject + am / is / are + not + V4 + Object

You are not watching TV.

आप TV नहीं देख रहे हो।

You are not studying nowadays.

आप आजकल पढ़ाई नहीं कर रहे हो।

You are not speaking English.

आप English नहीं बोल रहे हो।

You are not attending the class.

आप class attend नहीं कर रहे हो।

The child is not crying.

बच्चा नहीं रो रहा है।

The teacher is not explaining the subject.

शिक्षक विषय नहीं समझा रहे हैं।

The birds are not flying in the sky.

पक्षी आसमान में नहीं उड़ रहे हैं।

My father is not talking on the phone.

मेरे पापा phone पर बात नहीं कर रहे हैं।

Negative shortcut

बस helping verb के बाद not जोड़ना है:

I am reading. → I am not reading.

She is cooking. → She is not cooking.

They are playing. → They are not playing.

Lesson takeaway

Present Continuous को पहचानने और बनाने का सबसे simple path:

1. Action present में चल रहा है? → Present Continuous.

2. Subject पहचानो.

3. I → am, singular → is, plural/you/we/they → are.

4. Main verb → -ing form.

5. Negative में not; question में helping verb आगे.
