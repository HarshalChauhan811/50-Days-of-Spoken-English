Core idea

Tense = Time. Tense tells us whether an action is related to the present, past, or future.

इस lesson का main goal है पहले पूरे tense system का map समझना और फिर Present Indefinite को detail में master करना.

Why 12 types?

Time के 3 main divisions हैं: Present, Past, Future.

हर time में action को 4 ways से express किया जाता है: Indefinite, Continuous, Perfect, Perfect Continuous.

3 × 4 = 12 tense forms.

3. Tense पहचानने की सबसे पहली Skill

Common mistake

Hindi meaning से tense identify करके गलत helping verb लगा देना बहुत common mistake है.

“मैं जा रहा था” → I am going ❌

“मैं जा रहा था” → I was going ✅

“जा रहा था” past + continuous idea देता है.

Past Continuous → was / were + V4

I was going.

“मैं जा रहा हूँ” present continuous है.

Present Continuous → am / is / are + V4

I am going.

Golden identification rule

पहले time पहचानो → फिर tense type पहचानो → फिर उसकी helping verb चुनो → फिर main verb का सही form लगाओ.

4. Present Indefinite — Basic Concept

Present Indefinite = Simple Present

जब sentence किसी habit, routine, universal truth, fact या general information को express करता है, lesson में उसे Present Indefinite के रूप में लिया गया है.

1. Habit

I go for a walk every day.

मैं रोज walk पर जाता हूँ।

2. Routine

I wake up at 5 o'clock.

मैं 5 बजे उठता हूँ।

3. Universal Truth

The sun rises in the east.

सूर्य पूर्व में उदय होता है।

4. Fact / Information

Virat Kohli plays cricket.

विराट कोहली cricket खेलता है।

Traditional Hindi पहचान

अक्सर Hindi sentences के अंत में ता है / ती है / ते हैं जैसे forms मिलते हैं: जाता है, खाती है, खेलते हैं.

5. Present Indefinite — Positive Structure

Main structure

Subject + V1 + Object

लेकिन singular subject (he, she, it, singular name) के साथ main verb में S / ES लगता है.

Subject TypeRuleExample
I / We / You / TheyV1I go. / We play. / They speak.
He / She / It / Singular NameV1 + S / ESHe goes. / She speaks. / Raju plays.

I go. = मैं जाता हूँ।

I eat. = मैं खाता हूँ।

I run. = मैं भागता हूँ।

I sleep. = मैं सोता हूँ।

She goes. = वह जाती है।

She eats. = वह खाती है।

6. S vs ES — Exact Rule

When to use ES

Lesson rule: अगर verb के end में s, sh, x, o, ch आए, तो singular subject के साथ generally ES लगाया जाता है.

VerbReason3rd Person Singular
watchends with chwatches
goends with ogoes
playnone of the listed endingsplays
watchch → ESHe watches.
Quick memory

S / SH / X / O / CH → ES

Otherwise → S (as taught in this lesson).

8. Present Indefinite + Infinitive (Purpose)

Purpose pattern

Subject + V1/V1+s + ... + to + V1

यहाँ to + V1 purpose यानी “करने के लिए” का idea देता है.

He begs to eat.

वह खाने के लिए भीख मांगता है।

I eat to live.

मैं जीने के लिए खाता हूँ।

We go to the office to earn money.

हम पैसे कमाने के लिए office जाते हैं।

They cut trees to live in the forest.

वे जंगल में रहने के लिए पेड़ काटते हैं।

9. Daily Routine — Present Indefinite का Natural Use

Daily routine structure

Subject + V1/V1+s + Object / Time / Place

I wake up at 5 o'clock.

I go for a walk.

I take a bath.

I get ready.

Model daily-routine flow from the lesson

Every morning, I wake up at 5:30. After that, I get fresh. Then I brush my teeth. Then I change my clothes. Then I go to play cricket because I feel that I should exercise.

I call my friends and we all gather at the ground. We play cricket for around two hours. I come back at 8 o'clock. Then I take a bath. After taking a bath, I worship.

After that, I have my breakfast. After having breakfast at 9 o'clock, I go to the office, where I meet my teammates. Then I shoot some videos and read a novel. I attend meetings during the day.

After that, I have my lunch. After having lunch, I rest for some time. Then I start working with my teammates and write content at the office.

After writing content for my new books, I take classes in the evening. After taking classes in the evening, I go back home and have my dinner. After having dinner, I spend time with my family. Before sleeping, I read some novels, and then I go to bed.

Routine connectors

Every morning • after that • then • during the day • after having • before sleeping • at 5 o'clock

10. Present Indefinite — Negative Sentences

Structure

Subject + do / does + not + V1 + Object

Negative sentence में S / ES हट जाता है क्योंकि do/does already subject-verb agreement carry करता है.

SubjectHelping VerbExample
I / We / You / Theydo not / don'tI don't go.
He / She / It / Singular Namedoes not / doesn'tHe doesn't help me.

I do not talk to her.

मैं उससे बात नहीं करता हूँ।

We don't go there.

हम वहाँ नहीं जाते हैं।

You don't learn English.

तुम English नहीं सीखते हो।

He does not help me.

वह मेरी मदद नहीं करता है।

They don't read books.

वे किताबें नहीं पढ़ते हैं।

Very important

He doesn't helps me. ❌

He doesn't help me. ✅

Does/doesn't के बाद हमेशा lesson rule के अनुसार V1.

More examples

I don't go to school.

I don't drink water.

He doesn't talk to me.

I don't go to meet him.

I don't eat food.

I don't wake up early.

11. Interrogative Sentences

Structure

Do / Does + Subject + V1 + Object?

Does she cook food?

क्या वह खाना बनाती है?

Does he speak English?

क्या वह English बोलता है?

Do you take tea?

क्या तुम चाय पीते हो?

Do children make noise?

क्या बच्चे शोर मचाते हैं?

Do they go to roam every Sunday?

क्या वे हर Sunday घूमने जाते हैं?

Question rule

Question में Do/Does आगे आता है और main verb V1 में रहती है.

Does she cook? ✅   |   Does she cooks? ❌

13. Double Actions / Connected Sentences

जब एक ही subject के साथ दो actions जुड़ते हैं, Present Indefinite में दोनों verbs को subject के हिसाब से form किया जाता है.

She cooks food and serves it.

He reads books and makes notes.

I wake up early and do exercise.

They go to school and play.

Teachers teach and ask questions.

14. DO / DOES for Emphasis

Emphasis / stress

Source lesson में do / does को कभी-कभी positive sentence में emphasis देने के लिए दिखाया गया है.

I do believe in hard work.

He does teach daily.

I do understand your point.

She does help her mother.

यह use normal positive structure से अलग है. इसका purpose यहाँ जोर देना / emphasis है, न कि ordinary affirmative sentence बनाना.

4 Core Uses

Habit → I walk every day.

Routine → I wake up at 5.

Universal truth → The sun rises in the east.

Fact / information → He plays cricket.

Top 5 mistakes to avoid
  1. I am go. ❌ → I am going. ✅ (Present Continuous)
  2. I am going. ❌ for “मैं जा रहा था” → I was going. ✅
  3. He go. ❌ → He goes. ✅
  4. He doesn't goes. ❌ → He doesn't go. ✅
  5. Does she cooks? ❌ → Does she cook? ✅
