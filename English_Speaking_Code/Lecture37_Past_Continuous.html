PAST CONTINUOUSWAS / WERE V4 / INGPOSITIVE NEGATIVEINTERROGATIVE WH-QUESTIONS
Core idea

Past Continuous is used when an action was continuing / in progress at a time in the past.

जब किसी past time पर कोई व्यक्ति कोई काम कर रहा था / चल रहा था, तब Past Continuous का use किया जाता है.

I was teaching. • You were writing. • They were going.

1. Past Continuous की पहचान

Common Hindi sense: रहा था / रही थी / रहे थे.

मैं पढ़ा रहा था। → I was teaching.

आप लिख रहे थे। → You were writing.

वे कहीं जा रहे थे। → They were going.

I saw you yesterday. You were going to the market.

मैंने कल तुम्हें देखा था। तुम market जा रहे थे।

I was talking on the phone at that time.

मैं उस समय phone पर बात कर रहा था।

I was studying.

मैं पढ़ाई कर रहा था।

2. Main Structure

Positive Structure

Subject + was / were + V4 (verb + ing) + Object

Lesson terminology: V4 = verb का -ing form.

SubjectHelping VerbExample
I / He / She / It / Singular NamewasI was reading. / He was reading. / Sita was reading.
We / You / They / PluralwereWe were reading. / You were reading. / They were reading.
Memory formula

I → WAS   |   He/She/It/Name/Singular → WAS

We/You/They/Plural → WERE

3. Subject पहचानो, फिर WAS/WERE चुनो

Sentence में पूछें: “कौन काम कर रहा था?” जो answer मिले, वही subject है.

SubjectCorrectExample
IwasI was teaching.
He / She / ItwasShe was dancing.
Singular NamewasRam was reading.
We / You / TheywereThey were playing.
Plural SubjectwereStudents were studying.

4. Positive Sentences — Step by Step

मैं पढ़ा रहा था.

I → was → teach + ing

I was teaching.

हम पढ़ रहे थे.

We → were → read + ing

We were reading.

आप English बोल रहे थे.

You → were → speak + ing

You were speaking English.

I was playing a game.

I was dancing.

I was laughing.

I was teaching.

We were studying.

5. Plural Subjects + WERE

The kids were playing in the ground.

बच्चे ground में खेल रहे थे।

They were going.

वे जा रहे थे।

You were speaking English.

आप English बोल रहे थे।

We were reading.

हम पढ़ रहे थे।

Kids, students, boys, girls, people जैसे plural subjects के साथ were use करें.

6. Positive Practice

  1. राम और श्याम झगड़ रहे थे.
  2. Police चोर का पीछा कर रही थी.
  3. तुम लोग मस्ती कर रहे थे.
  4. वह mobile चला रही थी.
  5. वह नाच रही थी.
Answers
1. Ram and Shyam were quarrelling.
2. The police were chasing the thief.
3. You people were making fun.
4. She was using her mobile.
5. She was dancing.

Two subjects joined by “and” are treated as plural in this lesson: Ram and Shyam were...

7. Negative Sentences

Structure

Subject + was / were + not + V4 + Object

She was not reading a book.

वह किताब नहीं पढ़ रही थी।

We were not riding a bike.

हम bike नहीं चला रहे थे।

We were not driving a car.

हम car नहीं चला रहे थे।

He was not talking to me.

वह मुझसे बात नहीं कर रहा था।

They were not preparing for school.

वे school की तैयारी नहीं कर रहे थे।

I was not helping my parents.

मैं अपने parents की मदद नहीं कर रहा था।

You were not going.

आप नहीं जा रहे थे।

You were not calling me.

आप मुझे phone नहीं कर रहे थे।

You were not swimming in the river.

आप नदी में तैर नहीं रहे थे।

Negative formula

was/were + not + V4

He was talking. → He was not talking.

They were playing. → They were not playing.

Were you taking a bath?

क्या आप नहा रहे थे?

10. Interrogative Negative

Structure

Was / Were + Subject + not + V4 + Object?

Was she not speaking the truth?

क्या वह सच नहीं बोल रही थी?

Were they not capturing pictures?

क्या वे pictures नहीं खींच रहे थे?

Was she not coming on time?

क्या वह समय पर नहीं आ रही थी?

क्या बच्चे शोर नहीं मचा रहे थे?

Was I not messaging you?

क्या मैं तुम्हें message नहीं कर रहा था?

11. WH-Questions

Structure

WH-word + was / were + Subject + V4 + Object?

What

What were you doing?

आप क्या कर रहे थे?

Why

Why was she writing?

वह क्यों लिख रही थी?

Where

Where was she going?

वह कहाँ जा रही थी?

How

How were you eating?

आप कैसे खा रहे थे?

When

When were you eating?

आप कब खा रहे थे?

Whom

Whom were you talking to?

आप किससे बात कर रहे थे?

What were you doing yesterday?

तुम कल क्या कर रहे थे?

Why was he crying in the school?

वह school में क्यों रो रहा था?

What were they playing in the playground?

वे playground में क्या खेल रहे थे?

Whom were you talking to at that time?

तुम उस समय किससे बात कर रहे थे?

Where was she going?

वह कहाँ जा रही थी?

WH-word note

Who को इस lesson में अलग structure की category बताया गया है, इसलिए यहाँ मुख्य practice What, Why, Where, How, When, Whom पर है.

12. One Verb, Many WH-Questions

Verb: EAT → EATING

What were you eating? = आप क्या खा रहे थे?

Why were you eating? = आप क्यों खा रहे थे?

Where were you eating? = आप कहाँ खा रहे थे?

How were you eating? = आप कैसे खा रहे थे?

When were you eating? = आप कब खा रहे थे?

एक ही helping-verb pattern में अलग-अलग WH-words लगाकर बहुत सारे questions frame किए जा सकते हैं.

13. Translation Practice — Family Scene

Hindi Paragraph

कल शाम मैं अपने कमरे में पढ़ रहा था। मेरी बहन बाहर खेल रही थी। माँ रसोई में खाना बना रही थी। पापा अखबार पढ़ रहे थे। बारिश भी हो रही थी। इसलिए हम घर के अंदर ही थे। मेरा दोस्त मुझे phone कर रहा था, लेकिन मैं उस समय अपना homework पूरा कर रहा था। TV पर एक अच्छी film चल रही थी। हम सब अपने-अपने काम कर रहे थे।

Model Translation

Yesterday evening, I was reading in my room. My sister was playing outside. Mother was cooking in the kitchen. Father was reading the newspaper. It was also raining, so we were inside the house.

My friend was calling me, but I was completing my homework at that time. A good movie was playing on TV. We were all doing our own work.

14. Speaking Strategy — Think → Translate → Speak

जो भी situation आपके सामने दिखे, उसे English में translate करने की कोशिश करें.

अपने mind में आने वाले thoughts को English में बोलने की habit बनाएं.

जो sentence नहीं आता, उसे note करें → बाद में उसका translation देखें → अगले दिन वही sentence बोलें.

Self-practice challenge

अगले 8–15 दिनों तक daily situations को पहले English में frame करने की कोशिश करें. जितना बोलेंगे, उतना Past Continuous का structure natural होगा.

Fast memory map

Past + action in progress → WAS/WERE + V4

I / He / She / It / Singular → WAS

We / You / They / Plural → WERE

Negative → was/were + not + V4

Question → was/were first

WH → WH + was/were + subject + V4

Final takeaway

Past Continuous = WAS/WERE + V4 (ING)

Past में ongoing action के लिए:

I/He/She/It/Name → was   |   We/You/They/Plural → were
