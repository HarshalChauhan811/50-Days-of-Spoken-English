Lecture 10 — There in the Past | Was / Were

There in the Past — Was / Were
Past Existence There + Was/Were Positive & Negative Questions WH Questions Vocabulary + Translation
Lecture Goal: Past में “कुछ था / कुछ थे” और “किसी जगह पर कुछ था/थे” को There + was/were से बनाना सीखना।

1. There in the Past — Main Idea

Past में There का प्रयोग मुख्य रूप से तब किया गया है जब हमें बताना हो कि कुछ था / कुछ थे, या किसी जगह पर कोई चीज मौजूद थी.

Use 1

जब sentence में अलग subject नहीं दिया गया हो और केवल existence बतानी हो.

There was a cow.

Use 2

जब किसी place/container में कुछ था या कुछ थे.

There were fish in the pond.

There + Was / Were + Object

3. There + Was/Were + Object + RPS

जब sentence में place या बाकी information भी दी हो, तो pattern होगा:

There + Was / Were + Object + RPS
RPS = Remaining Parts of Sentence
HindiEnglish
तालाब में मछलियां थीं।There were fish in the pond.
घर पर मेहमान थे।There were guests at home.
दुनिया में 200 देश थे।There were 200 countries in the world.
कक्षा में 50 विद्यार्थी थे।There were 50 students in the class.
बगीचे में बहुत सारे फूल थे।There were many flowers in the garden.
मेरे बैग में एक पेन था।There was a pen in my bag.
कमरे में दो खिड़कियां थीं।There were two windows in the room.
कल आकाश में बादल थे।There were clouds in the sky yesterday.
टेबल पर एक किताब थी।There was a book on the table.
Sentence-Building Steps: Place पहचानो → Object पहचानो → singular/plural तय करो → was/were लगाओ → RPS जोड़ो → पूरा sentence बोलो.

5. Negative Sentences

जब कहना हो कि कोई चीज नहीं थी / नहीं थीं, lecture में no के साथ यह pattern दिया गया:

There + Was / Were + No + Noun + RPS
HindiEnglish
मेरे मोबाइल में बैलेंस नहीं था।There was no balance in my mobile.
खिड़की पर पर्दे नहीं थे।There were no curtains on the windows.
रेफ्रिजरेटर में फल नहीं थे।There were no fruits in the refrigerator.
सड़क पर गाड़ियां नहीं थीं।There were no vehicles on the road.
Important distinction: “उस दिन मौसम अच्छा नहीं था” को lecture में There से नहीं बनाया गया। यहाँ weather subject है:

The weather was not good that day.

6. Interrogative Sentences

जब “क्या … था/थे?” पूछना हो, तो was/were को आगे लाते हैं.

Was / Were + There + Object + RPS?
HindiEnglish
क्या कमरे में कोई था?Was there anyone in the room?
क्या मेज पर एक किताब थी?Was there any book on the table?
क्या पार्क में लोग थे?Were there people in the park?
क्या पार्टी में नाच-गाना था?Was there dancing and singing in the party?
क्या गाड़ी में पेट्रोल था?Was there petrol in the vehicle?
Remember: Question में भी was/were object के हिसाब से select होगा.

7. Interrogative Negative

जब “क्या … नहीं था/थे?” पूछना हो:

Was / Were + There + No + Noun + RPS?
HindiEnglish
क्या अस्पताल में डॉक्टर नहीं था?Was there no doctor in the hospital?
क्या कंप्यूटर में समस्या नहीं थी?Was there no problem in the computer?
क्या पार्टी में खाना नहीं था?Was there no food in the party?
क्या बॉक्स में खिलौने नहीं थे?Were there no toys in the box?
क्या रिपोर्ट में गलती नहीं थी?Was there no mistake in the report?

8. WH Questions

जब amount, number, reason, choice आदि पूछना हो, WH word सबसे आगे आता है:

WH + Was / Were + There + Object + RPS?
How much

How much money was there in the locker?

Amount / uncountable quantity

How many

How many books were there in the bag?

Number / countable plural

HindiEnglish
तिजोरी में कितना पैसा था?How much money was there in the locker?
बैग में कितनी किताबें थीं?How many books were there in the bag?
कंप्यूटर में कौन-सी फाइल थी?Which file was there in the computer?
कक्षा में कितनी कुर्सियां थीं?How many chairs were there in the class?
हॉस्पिटल में कितने मरीज थे?How many patients were there in the hospital?
WH Reminder: How much → amount/uncountable   |   How many → countable plural number.

11. Translation Practice — Paragraph

Hindi Paragraph
पिछले वर्ष हमारे शहर में एक सुंदर बगीचा था। उसमें कई ऊंचे पेड़ और रंग-बिरंगे फूल थे। बगीचे के बीच में एक छोटा सा फव्वारा था। बच्चों और आगंतुकों के लिए झूले और बेंचें थीं। हर शाम वहां बहुत से लोग टहलते थे और ताजी हवा का आनंद लेते थे। पेड़ों पर पक्षी मधुर गाते थे। वहां का वातावरण शांत और स्वच्छ था। इसलिए वह खाली समय बिताने के लिए कई परिवारों की पसंदीदा जगह थी।

Model Translation

Last year there was a beautiful garden near our city.

There were many tall trees and colorful flowers in it.

There was a fountain in the middle of the garden.

There were swings and benches for the children and visitors.

Every evening many people used to walk there and used to enjoy fresh air.

The birds used to sing sweetly on the trees.

The environment there was peaceful and clean.

Therefore, it was a favorite place for many families to spend their free time.

Speaking Practice: Paragraph को पहले देखकर बोलो, फिर बिना देखे बोलने की कोशिश करो. Lecture में direct speaking practice पर जोर दिया गया.
