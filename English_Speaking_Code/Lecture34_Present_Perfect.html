Core idea

Present Perfect is used in the lesson for an action that has been completed or has just/recently been completed.

जब कोई काम हो चुका है / कर लिया है / complete हो गया है, तब Present Perfect का use किया जाता है.

I have eaten food. • He has gone to school. • They have forgiven us.

1. Present Perfect को पहचानें

Common Hindi sense

ऐसे phrases: चुका है, चुकी है, चुके हैं, किया है, कर लिया है, हो चुका है.

मैं खाना खा चुका हूँ। → I have eaten food.

मैंने पानी पी लिया है। → I have drunk water.

हम इंदौर पहुँच चुके हैं। → We have reached Indore.

Position in the tense map

Present के चार major types में Present Perfect तीसरा form है:

Present Indefinite → Present Continuous → Present Perfect → Present Perfect Continuous

2. Main Structure

Positive Structure

Subject + has / have + V3 + Object

SubjectHelping VerbExample
He / She / It / Singular NamehasHe has eaten.
I / We / You / TheyhaveI have eaten.
Memory rule

Singular → HAS   |   I / We / You / They → HAVE

और इसके बाद main verb का V3 (third form).

3. Positive Sentences — Step by Step

मैंने पानी पी लिया है.

Subject = I → have → drink का V3 = drunk

I have drunk water.

हमने पढ़ाई कर ली है.

Subject = we → have → study का V3 = studied

We have studied.

उसने तुम्हें देख लिया है.

Subject = he → has → see का V3 = seen

He has seen you.

उन्होंने मुझे बता दिया है.

Subject = they → have → tell का V3 = told

They have told me.

हम इंदौर पहुँच चुके हैं.

We have reached Indore.

5. Confusing Verb — READ

Spelling same, pronunciation changes

read – read – read की spelling same रहती है, लेकिन pronunciation context के अनुसार बदलती है.

FormPronunciationExample
V1read /रीड/I read books.
V2read /रेड/I read the book yesterday.
V3read /रेड/I have read the book.

Lesson reminder: forget = भूलना, जबकि forgive = माफ करना. दोनों को confuse न करें.

6. Negative Sentences

Structure

Subject + has / have + not + V3 + Object

not हमेशा helping verb के बाद आता है.

We have not watched the movie.

हमने film नहीं देखी है।

I have not lost my bag.

मैंने अपना bag नहीं खोया है।

They have not passed the exam.

उन्होंने exam pass नहीं किया है।

He has not reached home yet.

वह अभी तक घर नहीं पहुँचा है।

We have not answered him.

हमने उसे कोई जवाब नहीं दिया है।

Pattern to remember

Positive: He has reached home.

Negative: He has not reached home.

7. Interrogative Sentences

Structure

Has / Have + Subject + V3 + Object?

Have they gone abroad?

क्या वे विदेश जा चुके हैं?

Have we passed the exam?

क्या हमने परीक्षा पास कर ली है?

Have you sold your car?

क्या तुमने अपनी car बेच दी है?

Have you bought a new phone?

क्या आपने नया phone खरीदा है?

Has he sung a song?

क्या वह गाना गा चुका है?

Question formula

Helping verb आगे → Subject → V3 → Object → ?

8. Interrogative Negative

Structure

Has / Have + Subject + not + V3 + Object?

Has Raj not bought a new mobile?

क्या राज ने नया mobile नहीं खरीदा है?

Have we not followed all the instructions?

क्या हमने सभी instructions का पालन नहीं किया है?

Has the doctor not checked the patient?

क्या doctor ने patient की जाँच नहीं की है?

Have the guests not eaten food?

क्या guests ने खाना नहीं खाया है?

Has he not given you an answer?

क्या उसने तुम्हें जवाब नहीं दिया है?

9. WH-Questions

Structure

WH-word + has / have + Subject + V3 + Object?

क्यों, कहाँ, कब, कैसे, किससे आदि पूछने के लिए WH-word को शुरुआत में रखते हैं.

HindiEnglish

10. Five Forms from One Sentence

Base sentence: “मैंने खाना खा लिया है.”

1. I have eaten food.

2. I have not eaten food.

3. Have I eaten food?

4. Have I not eaten food?

5. Why have I eaten food?

Second example: “मैंने पढ़ाई कर ली है.”

1. I have studied.

2. I have not studied.

3. Have I studied?

4. Have I not studied?

इसी pattern को अलग-अलग verbs के साथ बोलकर practice करें.

11. Present Perfect Paragraph Practice

Morning routine — Present Perfect

Lesson takeaway

Present Perfect का सबसे important formula:

HAS / HAVE + V3

काम complete हो चुका है → I have done it.

काम complete नहीं हुआ है → I have not done it.

क्या काम complete हुआ है? → Have I done it?
