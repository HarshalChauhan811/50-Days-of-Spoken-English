Lecture 08 — Will Have

Lecture 08 mein Present → Past → Future progression ko complete kiya gaya hai. Pehle have / has, phir had, aur ab Future mein possession ke liye will have padha gaya. Saath hi positive, negative, interrogative, interrogative-negative, WH questions aur paragraph translation ki practice karayi gayi.

Lecture Blueprint

Simple → Infinitive → Negative → Interrogative → Interrogative Negative → WH Questions

1. Present, Past & Future — One Framework

English ke kisi bhi major topic ko teen time frames mein samajhna useful hai: Present, Past aur Future. Isi method ko Be Verb aur Have/Has ke saath follow kiya gaya.

TimeHave-family FormExample
PresentHave / HasI have a car.
PastHadI had a car.
FutureWill HaveI will have a car.
Why Complete All Three?

Agar Present aur Past clear hai lekin Future clear nahi hai, to future sentence bolte waqt confusion ho sakta hai. Isliye ek hi topic ko teen time frames mein practise karna important hai.

2. Today's Main Topic — Will Have

Jab humein batana ho ki future mein kisi person ke paas koi object / thing hogi, tab will have ka use karte hain.

Definition

WILL HAVE = Future mein kisi ke paas koi cheez hona

Basic Structure

Subject + WILL HAVE + Object

Simple Examples

I will have a car. — मेरे पास कार होगी।

I will have a house. — मेरे पास घर होगा।

Important

Will have सभी subjects के साथ same रहता है—singular और plural दोनों के साथ.

3. Future Possession — Five Basic Sentences

HindiEnglish

One-Line Rule

Future possession → Subject + WILL HAVE + Object

5. Infinitive — “करने के लिए”

Ab Future possession ke saath purpose batane ke liye to + Verb (First Form) use kiya gaya. Isse sentence thoda longer aur more useful ban jata hai.

Infinitive Formula

Subject + Will Have + Object + to + Verb (First Form)

Sentence Building Trick

Bada sentence ho to pehle main possession sentence banao, phir to + verb first form se purpose add karo.

6. Infinitive — Examples

HindiEnglish
उसके पास रहने के लिए घर होगा।He will have a house to live.
मेरे पास खर्च करने के लिए पैसे होंगे।I will have money to spend.
हमारे पास लड़ने के लिए हथियार होंगे।We will have weapons to fight.
तुम्हारे पास काम करने के लिए मशीन होगी।You will have a machine to work.
Infinitive Rule

TO + Verb's First Form

to live   |   to spend   |   to fight   |   to work

7. Future Negative — Will Not Have

Jab future mein kisi ke paas koi cheez nahi hogi, to will not have ka use hota hai.

Negative Formula

Subject + WILL NOT HAVE + Object

Common Mistake

“Will have” ko ek single block ki tarah chipkana nahi hai. Negative mein not beech mein aata hai:

WILL + NOT + HAVE

8. Future Negative — Examples

HindiEnglish
उस समय मेरे पास फोन नहीं होगा।I will not have a phone at that time.
हमारे पास अगले महीने फंड नहीं होगा।We won't have funds next month.
कंपनी के पास नए projects नहीं होंगे।The company won't have new projects.
उसके पास register नहीं होगा।He will not have a register.
आपके पास अगले सप्ताह समय नहीं होगा।You will not have time next week.
Short Form

Will not → Won't

We will not have funds. → We won't have funds.

10. Interrogative — Will + Have

Future possession ka question banane ke liye will ko beginning mein le aate hain.

Question Formula

WILL + Subject + HAVE + Object?

HindiEnglish
क्या बच्चों के पास नए खिलौने होंगे?Will children have new toys?
क्या मेरे दोस्तों के पास नौकरी होगी?Will my friends have a job?
क्या तुम्हारे पास टिकट होगा?Will you have tickets?
क्या उसके पास पैसे होंगे?Will he have money?
क्या हमारे पास समय होगा?Will we have time?
Transformation

You will have tickets.

→ Will you have tickets?

11. Interrogative Negative — Will + Not + Have

Question + Negative Formula

WILL + Subject + NOT + HAVE + Object?

HindiEnglish
क्या छात्रों के पास उत्तर नहीं होगा?Will students not have an answer?
क्या राम के पास समय नहीं होगा?Will Ram not have time?
क्या राहुल के पास कल तक पैसे नहीं होंगे?Will Rahul not have money by tomorrow?
क्या शिक्षक के पास समय नहीं होगा?Will the teacher not have time?
क्या सैनिकों के पास हथियार नहीं होंगे?Will soldiers not have weapons?
Formation Order

WILL → Subject → NOT → HAVE → Object?

12. WH-Questions — Future Possession

WH-question mein pehle WH word, phir will, phir subject, aur phir have aata hai.

What
When
Why
Where
Which
Who
How
Whose
WH Formula

WH + WILL + Subject + HAVE + ...?

HindiEnglish
तुम्हारे पास कल क्या होगा?What will you have tomorrow?
उनके पास नया मोबाइल कब होगा?When will they have a new mobile?
तुम्हारे पास नई किताब क्यों होगी?Why will you have a new book?
उसके पास इतना समय क्यों होगा?Why will he have that much time?
उनके पास नया घर कब होगा?When will they have a new house?
WH Rule

WH word ke baad will aata hai. Subject uske baad, aur have subject ke baad.

What + will + you + have?

14. Paragraph Translation — Hindi → English

Ab Future possession ko ek connected paragraph ke andar apply kiya gaya. Main aim hai individual sentences se aage badhkar continuous translation karna.

Paragraph — Model Translation

अगले वर्ष मेरे पास नई साइकिल होगी।

Next year I will have a new bicycle.

मेरे पास पढ़ाई के लिए नई किताबें भी होंगी।

I will have new books to study.

परीक्षा के बाद मेरे पास अधिक समय होगा।

I will have more time after the exam.

उस समय मेरे पास कई नए अनुभव होंगे।

At that time I will have many new experiences.

मेरे परिवार के पास एक अच्छी योजना होगी।

My family will have a good plan.

भविष्य में हमारे पास सफलता पाने के अधिक अवसर होंगे।

In the future we will have many chances to get success.

Translation Method

Paragraph ko sentence-by-sentence break karo, future time identify karo, phir will have ya will have + to-infinitive choose karo.
