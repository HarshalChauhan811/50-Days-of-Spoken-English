Lecture Focus

This lecture covers Past Perfect and Past Perfect Continuous with simple spoken-English logic, structures, examples, negative sentences, questions, WH-questions, Since / For, and practice.

1. English Thinking: Translation → Direct English

Translation Method

Beginning learners often think in the mother tongue first and then convert the thought into English.

Hindi thought → English
जाने → go   |   आने → come   |   बैठने → sit   |   बात करने → talk

Direct English

With enough vocabulary, repeated sentence practice, and speaking habits, familiar sentences start coming directly in English.

Example: May I come in? becomes automatic after repeated use.
Daily Routine Practice: I wake up early in the morning. Then I brush my teeth. Then I go for a walk. Then I have breakfast. Then I get ready. Then I go to school.
Key idea: Translation is a support at the beginning; repeated practice helps you develop the habit of thinking in English.

3. Past Perfect

Meaning

For spoken understanding, think of Past Perfect as an action that had already been completed in the past.

“चुका था”“कर लिया था”Past completion
Structure: Subject + had + V3 + Object
Hindi IdeaEnglish
वह इंदौर पहुँच चुका था।He had reached Indore.
हम खाना खा चुके थे।We had eaten food.
उसने मुझे सब बता दिया था।He had told me everything.
उन्होंने पत्र पढ़ लिया था।They had read the letter.
वह यह किताब पढ़ चुका था।He had read this book.
Important: Past Perfect uses the same helping verb had with all subjects: I, you, he, she, it, we, they.

Past Perfect — Why V3?

The pattern is always had + V3. So verb forms are important.

V1V2V3
eatateeaten
givegavegiven
telltoldtold
readreadread
buyboughtbought
sellsoldsold
Pronunciation reminder: read has the same spelling in V1/V2/V3, but the pronunciation changes in the past: “reed” → “red”.

4. Past Perfect — Positive Practice

HindiEnglish
मैंने खाना खा लिया था।I had eaten food.
उन्होंने फिल्म देख ली थी।They had watched the movie.
तुमने किताब पढ़ ली थी।You had read the book.
हम दिल्ली पहुँच चुके थे।We had reached Delhi.
उसने दरवाज़ा बंद कर दिया था।He had closed the door.
5-sentence drill: Make each sentence using Subject + had + V3 + Object. First identify the subject; then choose the correct V3.

5. Past Perfect — Negative

Structure: Subject + had + not + V3 + Object
HindiEnglish
मैंने अपना काम पूरा नहीं किया था।I had not done my work.
बच्चों ने होमवर्क नहीं किया था।The children had not done the homework.
पुलिस ने चोर को नहीं पकड़ा था।The police had not caught the thief.
डॉक्टर ने मरीज की जाँच नहीं की थी।The doctor had not checked the patient.
राहुल ने अपनी गाड़ी नहीं बेची थी।Rahul had not sold his vehicle.
Pattern shortcut: बस had के बाद not लगा दो. Verb फिर भी V3 में रहेगा.

6. Past Perfect — Interrogative

Structure: Had + Subject + V3 + Object?
HindiEnglish
क्या वह पहुँच चुका था?Had he reached?
क्या उसने खाना खा लिया था?Had he eaten the food?
क्या माँ ने खाना बनाया था?Had Mother cooked the food?
क्या हमने सामान खरीद लिया था?Had we bought the goods?
क्या राहुल ने गाड़ी बेच दी थी?Had Rahul sold the vehicle?
क्या हम बारिश से पहले आ गए थे?Had we come before the rain?
क्या उसने मुझे बुला लिया था?Had he called me?

Interrogative Negative & WH Questions

Interrogative Negative

Had + Subject + not + V3 + Object?

Example: Had he not finished the work?

WH Question

WH-word + had + Subject + V3 + Object?

Example: What had he done?

7. Past Perfect vs Past Indefinite

The lecture notes that the same situation can sometimes be expressed in Past Indefinite without making the sentence grammatically impossible. The important learning goal here is to become comfortable with different ways of expressing past events.

Core distinction: Past Perfect is especially useful when a past action is being related to an earlier completed past action or when “already completed in the past” is the focus.
Timeline example:
First → The train had left.
Then → I reached the railway station.

As I reached the railway station, the train had gone.

8. Past Perfect Continuous

Meaning

Use it when an action had been continuing for some time in the past.

“रहा था + time”Past durationContinuing action
Structure: Subject + had been + V4/ING + Object + since/for + Time
HindiEnglish
वह 2 घंटे से मोबाइल चला रहा था।He had been using the mobile for 2 hours.
तुम 3 बजे से चिल्ला रहे थे।You had been shouting since 3 o’clock.
हम दो वर्षों से इस घर में रह रहे थे।We had been living in this house for 2 years.

9. Since vs For

Since = Starting Point

Use since when the beginning point is given.

since 3 o’clock
since Monday
since 3 April
since 2012
since morning

For = Duration

Use for when a period / length of time is given.

for 2 hours
for 4 days
for 5 months
for 10 years
for many days
Remember: Since = कब से / starting point    For = कितने समय से / duration

10. Past Perfect Continuous — Positive Practice

HindiEnglish
मैं 2 घंटे से अंग्रेज़ी पढ़ रहा था।I had been learning English for 2 hours.
वह सुबह से खाना बना रही थी।She had been cooking since morning.
वे कई दिनों से क्रिकेट खेल रहे थे।They had been playing cricket for many days.
हम रात से बारिश का इंतज़ार कर रहे थे।We had been waiting for the rain since night.
तुम दो साल से उस कंपनी में काम कर रहे थे।You had been working in that company for two years.

11. Past Perfect Continuous — Negative

Structure: Subject + had + not + been + V4/ING + Object + since/for + Time
HindiEnglish
बच्चे दोपहर से टीवी नहीं देख रहे थे।The kids had not been watching TV since noon.
वह कई महीनों से नौकरी नहीं ढूँढ रहा था।He had not been looking for a job for many months.
वे सुबह से स्कूल नहीं जा रहे थे।They had not been going to school since morning.
हम सालों से इस घर में नहीं रह रहे थे।We had not been living in this house for years.
वह एक घंटे से मुझसे बात नहीं कर रहा था।He had not been talking to me for an hour.

Interrogative & WH — Pattern Builder

Interrogative

Had + Subject + been + V4 + ...?

Had you been studying since morning?

Interrogative Negative

Had + Subject + not + been + V4 + ...?

Had he not been working for two years?

WH Question

WH-word + had + Subject + been + V4 + ...?

Since when had you been studying?

Useful WH phrase: “कब से?” → Since when?
